# Abstract: SDF Active Tensor Foam Framework

This research introduces the **QVEX** (Quantum Vacuum Exotic eXcitations) framework to model the dynamics of NGC 6503. We demonstrate that galactic rotation curves emerge not from dark matter, but from the hydrodynamic response of a 5-tetrahedral spacetime lattice—a Spacetime Dielectric Foam (SDF).

## Core Framework

- **Effective Tensor Field:** We define the effective stress-energy tensor as $T^{\mu\nu}_{eff} = T^{\mu\nu}_{bar} + T^{\mu\nu}_{foam}$.
- **Radial Unfreezing:** A transition mechanism $\xi(r)$ governs the vacuum response, where the "frozen" lattice at high accelerations (Newtonian core) undergoes phase liquefaction at the acceleration scale $a_0 \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-10} \text{ m/s}^2$, leading to mass-enhancement polarization.
- **Observational Outcome:** The framework recovers the Baryonic Tully-Fisher Relation (BTFR) with a slope of $\approx 4.0$ analytically, eliminating the need for dark matter halos.
